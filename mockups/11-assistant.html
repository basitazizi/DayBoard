Move my gym to tomorrow, and how does the electron transport chain make ATP?
Gym moved to Tue 6:00 PMUndo
Electrons from NADH and FADH₂ pass along proteins in the inner mitochondrial membrane. Each step pumps protons across, building a gradient. Protons flow back through ATP synthase, which uses that flow to make about 26–28 ATP. Oxygen takes the electrons at the end, forming water.
