Cell respiration

Overview

Cells turn glucose into usable energy (ATP) in three stages. Total yield is about 30–32 ATP per glucose.

1. Glycolysis

  • Happens in the cytoplasm, no oxygen needed
  • Glucose → 2 pyruvate, net gain of 2 ATP and 2 NADH

2. Krebs cycle

  • Happens in the mitochondrial matrix
  • Produces 2 ATP, 6 NADH, 2 FADH₂ and releases CO₂

3. Electron transport chain

  • Inner mitochondrial membrane, oxygen is the final acceptor
  • Makes the bulk of the ATP (about 26–28)
