FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------
DayBoard is the planner that tells you what to do next.

Classes, assignments, exams, tasks, habits and notes live in one place, and DayBoard puts them in order for you. Open it and you'll see what's up next, what's due, and where your free time is.

ADD ANYTHING IN ONE LINE
Type "Chem quiz Thursday at 2pm and history essay due Friday" and DayBoard creates both, on the right dates, linked to the right classes. You can also say it out loud, or take a photo of a syllabus or whiteboard. You always see what will be added before anything is saved.

YOUR DAY, IN ORDER
• Up next: your next class or event, with a countdown
• Today's plan: classes, study sessions, tasks and deadlines on one timeline
• A short daily brief of what matters right now

STUDY PLANS THAT MAKE THEMSELVES
Add an exam and DayBoard sets aside study sessions in your free time before it. Big tasks are broken into steps, so the first one always feels easy.

SCHOOL, SORTED
• Every class with its own colour, days and room
• Assignments and exams in one list, ranked by what's most urgent
• Enter your scores and see each class average

FOCUS AND HABITS
• A distraction-free focus timer with breaks and background sounds
• Habit check-ins, streaks and a five-week history
• Insights that show when you work best and what's slipping

AN ASSISTANT THAT KNOWS YOUR WEEK
Ask "What should I work on next?" or "When am I free today?" and get an answer from your own schedule.

MAKE IT YOURS
Light or dark mode, accent colours, fonts, backgrounds, a custom avatar and profile, and a dashboard you can rearrange card by card.

ALSO
• Reminders before things start
• Search everything in seconds
• Works without an account; sign in to sync your phone, laptop and wall display
• Download a backup of all your data at any time
• Delete your account from Settings whenever you want

DayBoard is free, has no ads, and never sells your information.
